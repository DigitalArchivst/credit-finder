2. Experimental status and the AS IS research disclaimer from SKILL.md.
3. The skill's MIT license and a separate report license statement.
4. A rights reservation for the reviewed work and third-party material.
5. Any relevant dependency or reproduction notices, with uncertainty preserved.

Do not ask the maker about creative origins or delay research merely because report licensing is unspecified. Use the no-new-license sentence and continue.

## Rights records

Record actual component versions and notices when available. Do not replace a version-specific notice with a current upstream notice without saying so. A credit line is not a complete software license notice unless the applicable terms say it is sufficient. Link or preserve the required notice as appropriate.

For images, figures, audio samples, video, and data, distinguish citing or linking from reproducing. Record the source, public creator, exact rights statement, access date, and any proposed modifications. Unknown permission stays unknown. Do not state that reuse is legally cleared, and do not infer that open access means unrestricted reuse.

## Disclaimer scope

The plain-language notice describes research limits. It does not replace, amend, or expand the MIT license or CC BY 4.0 legal code. It creates no guarantee that a warning prevents every error or claim. A disclaimer cannot repair an unsupported attribution; correct the finding itself.

## Short form for Quick mode

Quick reports use this short form in place of the full notice, report-license sentence, and rights sentence:

> Experimental output from Credit Finder v1.3.2 (MIT License). Provided AS IS, without warranty. This is research help, not a finding about legal rights, originality, or misconduct. No license is granted over this report, the reviewed work, or third-party material.

It keeps the same four points (experimental, MIT, AS IS, and no license over the report, the reviewed work, or third-party material) in fewer words. If an authorized license applies to the report's commentary, name it in place of "this report, ". Standard, Deep, offline, partial, and self-test reports keep the full form, because longer reports are more likely to be relied on or published.
