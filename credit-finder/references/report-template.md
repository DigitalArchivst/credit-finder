# CREDIT report template

Fill every required section. Remove instructional placeholders. Preserve the version, experimental status, license scope, and disclaimer in every mode. This template is MIT licensed; the report's own original commentary needs its own rights statement.

- [Unverified findings](#unverified-findings)

## Template

```markdown
# CREDIT report: <work>

Prepared with Credit Finder v1.3.2 on <YYYY-MM-DD>.
Report revision: <revision>. Submitted work: <version or file identity>.
Status: Experimental. <Complete within scope, or partial and why.>

## Summary

<Scope, point and finding counts, supported recommendations, limits.>
Earlier work and similarity do not establish use or intent.

## How this was done

- Mode: <quick, standard, deep; partial if applicable>
- Teams: <actual tracer/challenger/reviewer counts, or solo>
- Web access: <yes, or no; every offline finding is unverified>
- Media inspected: <actual channels and representations>
- Retrievals: <actual count or not measured> / <cap>
- Scope and exclusions: <languages, dates, areas, unexamined media>
- Review checks: <exact F-IDs checked and deferred; no challenge in Quick>
- Points: <selected/traced/unresolved counts; distinguish findings from points>

## Findings

| Finding | Point and locator | Creator and work | Group | Strength | Verification | Confidence | Evidence |

<If there are no findings, write "No findings" instead of an empty table.>

### No credit recommended

<Routine items and reasons, or None. This is not a credit group or strength.>

## Corrections to existing credits

<Evidence-supported corrections keyed to findings, or None found.>

## Side-by-side matches

## Unsettled items

<Unverified/disputed candidates, unresolved rights, missing sources and dates,
```

## Unverified findings

An uncertain finding must not appear as a factual credit in the paste-ready section. Keep its candidate attribution in Unsettled items. In offline mode, all findings remain (unverified); the paste-ready section can list citation tasks without asserting a candidate source.

See [license and disclaimer rules](license-and-disclaimer.md) for authorized CC BY 4.0 wording. No new sidecar file is required from the user; the deliverable stays one Markdown report.
